# AI_WORKFLOW — replace placeholders with your own experience

Short, specific answers are best. A few sentences each is enough.

1. **Tools:** Which development and AI tools did you use (or "none")?
2. **One delegated task:** Paste one prompt you gave an AI tool, as you wrote it. What did you keep, and what did you change?
3. **One problem you caught:** A bug, wrong assumption or bad suggestion (from an AI tool or yourself). What was the symptom, how did you notice, and how did you prove the fix worked (test, reproduction, or before/after numbers)?

Optional: approximate time spent. It helps us calibrate the assignment and does not affect your score.
